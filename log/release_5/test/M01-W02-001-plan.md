## Fixtures, oracle and execution discipline

Use fresh temporary output directories and synthetic model/control/measurement
objects. Preserve existing test_thermal_example.py assertions rather than
rewriting its numerical/storage contract. W01 oracle remains eight temperatures
20, 21, 21.9, 22.71, 23.439, 24.0951, 24.68559, 25.217031, absolute tolerance
1e-10, source tick 0–7 at acquisition tick 1–8; finite values and exact schema,
units, UUID identity, reopening and meaningful headless PNG are required.
Compare repeats excluding UUID/wall timestamps. Keep borrowed figures, default
scheduler and loops observable before/after; tests clean only owned resources.

Observe requests, acknowledgment and actual coordinator commits independently.
Use deterministic synchronous acquisition-boundary callbacks or documented test
seams selected by design, never a wall-clock sleep or probabilistic thread race.
Inject model errors in real evolution and sample errors in a real getter; count
completed rows and successful coordinator boundaries externally. Patching a
boundary or faulting dependency is acceptable; replacing the entire acquisition
with a mock is insufficient evidence. Exercise at K=3, N=8 and zero-row edges.
